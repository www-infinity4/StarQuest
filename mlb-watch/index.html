How the investigation works

One player record, several evidence layers

Baseball information is scattered across live data, official biographies, school archives, historical research, transactions, media guides, and statistical databases. MLB Watch joins the layers without hiding uncertainty.

01

Official identity

Name, birthplace, position, debut, handedness, draft information, and current status.

02

Life research

Hometown, family context, early baseball, schooling, college, military or international development.

03

Career ledger

Drafts, signings, organizations, teams, transactions, injuries, awards, milestones, and post-career work.

04

Statistical context

Current, season-by-season, career, postseason, advanced, and era-adjusted performance where licensing permits.

05

Source verification

Fact-level citations, publication date, source type, conflicts, corrections, and last review.

06

Human review

AI can organize evidence, but disputed biography, rights, discipline, and sensitive claims require human review.
